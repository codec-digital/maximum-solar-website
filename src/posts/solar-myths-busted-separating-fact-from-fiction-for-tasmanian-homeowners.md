Tasmania is leading the charge on renewables, but there's still a bit of confusion out there around solar power. If you've heard a tall tale or two, this is for you. Let's clear up the most common myths with genuine, Tasmania-tested facts.

## Myth 1: "Tasmania's Too Cloudy for Solar"

Wrong! While it's true the mainland clocks up slightly higher yearly sunlight, Tasmanian homes still get plenty of sun. Modern panels work brilliantly here, capturing energy even on overcast days.

Both Hobart and Launceston regularly see fantastic returns. Plus, the cooler climate actually helps boost panel efficiency.

## Myth 2: "Solar's Not Worth It Without Batteries"

Not at all. Batteries are great for storing excess power, but standard solar systems deliver incredible savings even without them. Exporting your surplus to the grid with a feed-in tariff still means cash back.

Add a battery when the time is right for you.

## Myth 3: "Solar is Too Expensive Upfront"

Solar's more affordable now than ever before. Thanks to Federal and State rebates, the average Tasmanian household can install a high-quality system for a fraction of the 2010 price.

Combine that with low running costs and quick payback, and solar's a top-value investment.

## Myth 4: "Warranties Aren't Reliable"

No longer true with reputable brands. Today's top manufacturers offer guaranteed performance for 25 years or more.

Look for CEC-accredited installers and panels with plenty of real, Aussie-based customer reviews.

## Myth 5: "Solar is High Maintenance"

Fact is, solar panels require almost no day-to-day upkeep. A basic inspection once a year and an occasional clean are all you need for a reliable, high-performing system.
