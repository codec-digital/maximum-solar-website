Australia is the world's solar superstar. No other country installs as much rooftop solar per person as we do. But why is Australia racing ahead and what does that mean for your family or business?

## The Stats Don't Lie

Australia now boasts more than three million rooftop solar systems. That means one in three households has gone solar. Across capital cities and the deepest country towns, our rooftops are transformed into power stations.

We've already beaten global heavyweights like Germany and the USA for panels per capita. In 2025, we remain the clear leader.

## Why Are We Out in Front?

- **Brilliant Sunshine**: Aussies enjoy some of the world's best solar conditions, with sunshine year-round and reliable output from nearly every major region.


- **Proactive Policy**: Smart federal and state incentives have helped cut costs and made solar attainable for millions.


- **High Power Prices**: With our traditionally high grid prices, solar just makes economic sense panels pay for themselves quickly through big savings.


- **Tech-Savvy Homeowners**: Australians love practical, cost-cutting solutions, and solar is both high-tech and dead simple once installed.


- **Green Values**: As a nation, we're serious about reducing emissions and building a cleaner, greener legacy for the next generation.



## Leadership Means Better Choices for You

Because Aussies buy so much solar, we get access to the latest panels and tech faster than most. Top brands flock to our market with premium warranties and support.

Plus, there's a wide pool of accredited installers so you're spoilt for choice and competitive prices.

## A Growing Future

Australia's solar growth isn't slowing down. More homes and businesses are adding batteries and smart controls, turning the humble rooftop into the backbone of a new energy future. International eyes are on us as the benchmark for clean energy leadership.
